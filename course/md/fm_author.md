# About the Author

**Srinivasa Rao Bittla** is a seasoned technology leader with over twenty years of expertise at the intersection of AI/ML, performance engineering, and quality assurance. He has held senior roles across performance engineering, AI/ML, and quality assurance at some of the world's leading technology companies.

His past experience includes Adobe, Veeva Systems, LogMeIn and Citrix, and founding Zest Bittla IT Solutions, where he mentored more than 10,000 professionals. He holds multiple patents in AI-enabled predictive analytics and blockchain security, and is a past IEEE AI policy contributor.

He is recognized as a Gatling Master and is a keynote speaker at AI testing conferences. His recognitions include the Symphony Hall of Fame, Adobe Team Excellence, and Stevie Gold, Noble Gold and Titan Gold awards.

His other books are *AI Performance Engineering*, *The Last Invention* and *AI-Driven Software Testing* (Apress).

More about his work and books is at **bittla.me**. The code for this book is at github.com/sbittla/building-agentic-ai.
